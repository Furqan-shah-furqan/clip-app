Delete your data

You can manage stored ClipFlow data yourself or ask Furqan Shah for help at furqanfff6@gmail.com.

Disconnect a provider

  1. Sign into your ClipFlow account.
  2. Open Account settings.
  3. Find the YouTube or Instagram connection.
  4. Choose Disconnect and confirm.

This removes ClipFlow's stored connection and associated schedules. Wait for active publishing to finish first. It does not remove posts already published or necessarily remove the provider's app permission.

Revoke provider permissions

For YouTube, remove ClipFlow using Google's third-party permissions settings. For Instagram's Facebook-login connection, remove ClipFlow in your Facebook Business Integrations settings. Reconnecting requires granting permission again.

Delete your ClipFlow account

  1. Finish active clip generation and publishing.
  2. Open Account settings while signed in.
  3. Enter your current ClipFlow password.
  4. Select the checkbox confirming that deletion cannot be undone.
  5. Choose Permanently delete account.

This removes your account, database records, sessions, connections and schedules, and cleans up identified local files and protected cloud videos. If cleanup fails, your account is locked to prevent new work; retry deletion from Account settings or contact support. Published YouTube/Instagram posts must be managed on those platforms.

Request help or specific data removal

Email furqanfff6@gmail.com with the subject ClipFlow data deletion request. State your account email and what you want removed. We may ask you to confirm ownership. Do not send passwords or tokens. Support will provide a confirmation or explain any remaining cleanup.

Infrastructure logs, backups, provider copies and temporary files on separate workers may have their own retention lifecycles. Contact support about follow-up removal. See the Privacy Policy for details.
