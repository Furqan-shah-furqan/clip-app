Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how Furqan Shah operates ClipFlow Studio and handles information when you use it. Contact furqanfff6@gmail.com with privacy questions.

Information we collect

  • Account information: the name and email you provide, a password hash, session records and password-recovery records.
  • Content and projects: source links, uploaded video/audio, clips, captions, editing settings, project metadata and processing-job records.
  • Connected accounts: provider account/channel identifiers and names, granted connection details, encrypted authorization tokens and token-expiry information.
  • Publishing information: the selected video, title, caption, hashtags, visibility where supported, schedule, publication result and error/attempt records.
  • Technical information: requests and operational logs used for security, troubleshooting and service operation. Requests may include your IP address and browser information.

How we use information

We use this information to sign you in, process the content you request, display and save your projects, recover your account, connect provider accounts, carry out your publishing instructions and troubleshoot problems. Connecting an account does not by itself authorize an immediate post; publishing uses the account and settings selected for your request or schedule.

Google and YouTube data

ClipFlow uses YouTube API Services to identify the channel you connect and upload/publish the videos you authorize. Google account data is used for these user-facing features, not for advertising profiles or training general-purpose AI models. We do not sell Google account data. Google handles its own data under the Google Privacy Policy.

You can remove the connection in Account settings. To revoke Google's remaining permission for ClipFlow, use Google's third-party permissions settings. Revocation may cause scheduled publishing to fail until you reconnect. Contact us to request removal of stored provider data.

Meta and Instagram data

For an eligible account, ClipFlow uses the permissions you grant to identify its connected Facebook Page/Instagram professional account and publish the videos you request. We retain connection identifiers, encrypted tokens and publishing records for these features. You can disconnect in ClipFlow and separately remove the app's permissions in the relevant Facebook/Instagram settings. Meta handles its own information under the Meta Privacy Policy.

Services that handle data

Render hosts the application and processing services. The configured PostgreSQL database stores account and publishing records; Redis handles background-job queues. Cloudinary stores uploaded/generated videos when cloud storage is configured. Resend handles recovery-email delivery. Google and Meta receive the content and metadata required for authorized publishing.

Supported YouTube-source retrieval may use Savenow.to or a configured RapidAPI download service, which receives the source URL and related requests. Caption transcription and clip processing use server-side tools, including local Whisper implementations. These services have their own policies and may process information in countries different from yours.

Storage and security

ClipFlow stores passwords as hashes and provider tokens encrypted on the backend. App routes enforce customer ownership. Customer-mode cloud uploads use authenticated delivery and signed video links. A person receiving a signed link may be able to use it; do not share links to private content. No service can guarantee absolute security.

Cookies and browser storage

A necessary session cookie keeps you signed in, normally for up to seven days. Recovery links expire after 30 minutes and are intended for one use. Browser storage saves preferences and working project/editor state. Signing into a different customer clears ClipFlow's previous-customer working state in that browser. Third-party services use their own cookies when you visit or authorize them.

Retention and deletion

Information is retained while needed for the features you use, until you delete it or request removal. Project-list entries older than approximately 31 days may be removed from the local list; this is not a promise that all related videos or records are automatically erased at that time.

Account deletion removes the customer's database records, sessions, connections and schedules, and attempts to remove identified local files and authenticated Cloudinary videos. Active generation/publishing must finish first. If cleanup fails, the account is locked and deletion can be retried. Temporary files in separate processing environments, infrastructure logs, provider-held data or backups may persist under their own lifecycles; contact us if removal needs follow-up. ClipFlow does not remove posts already published to YouTube or Instagram.

Your choices

You can update caption/project content, disconnect accounts, delete your ClipFlow account or email us to request access, correction or deletion of your information. We may ask you to verify ownership before acting. See Data Deletion for the steps. Do not send your password or provider tokens.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Contact the operator if you need clarification about how your information is handled.
